## Description

The one sentence every tool uses to say it held something back: what was kept, what exists, and the
exact call that fetches the rest. Silent truncation is the worse failure — a reader cannot tell a
complete answer from a clipped one, and will act on the clipped one — so no cut on the serving
path is allowed to be silent. Deliberately uniform and deliberately single-owner, for the same
reason as [[note-trust-reminder]]: a reader learns the shape once and then recognises it coming from
any tool.
